I recently graduated from [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/) with a Master's in [Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://www.ece.cmu.edu/), concentrating in AI/ML Systems, and I'm now based in the San Francisco Bay Area as a Machine Learning Engineer at [PerforatedAI](https://www.perforatedai.com/).

In the past, I completed my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at CMU with a minor in Machine Learning. As a graduate student, I worked with the Human Sensing Lab on designing a quantitative framework to measure modality bias in Video-Language Models. I was also the Lead TA for Ethics and Policy Issues in Computing at CMU in Fall 2025 and Spring 2026.

My research interests include Multimodal Machine Learning, Foundation Models and Explainable AI. I'm especially interested in understanding the shortcomings of Vision-Language Models so we can build better multimodal systems and AI agents.
