### TL;DR
We explain how to use Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) to generate a possible market for the price of cultivated Blueberries imported into the USA based on USDA data for the past 4 years. We performed experiments to find the best Kernel to fit the data, and use a Matern ($\nu = \frac{3}{2}$) Kernel to model the underlying process.
